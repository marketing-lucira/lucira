CaratLane — store network & catalog mix
Every store below is a real, publicly-listed CaratLane location. Pincode, address and city come from that store's own live catalog page on caratlane.com.
Read this before using the numbers. CaratLane does not publish real per-store physical stock anywhere public — no retailer does. The "inventory" figures here are online catalog listing counts pulled from each store's own filter sidebar on caratlane.com (a public proxy for local assortment depth/mix), not audited warehouse or shelf stock.
  • Every store page also lists more categories and more price bands than captured here — figures below are a floor, not the full catalog.
  • Coverage is 112 of an estimated ~230+ stores nationwide (the 7 metros alone list 148 stores on CaratLane's own city pages) — stores that 404'd during collection were skipped, never guessed.
  • "Diamond / Gold / Gemstone / Platinum" are material filters, not product categories — kept out of the category total to avoid double-counting a piece under both dimensions.
  • 2 stores (Phoenix Marketcity Viman Nagar-Pune, ECR Neelankarai-Chennai) show anomalously small catalog counts vs. their city peers — reported as fetched, not corrected, and flagged in the table.
