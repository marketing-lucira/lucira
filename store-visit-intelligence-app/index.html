Store Visit Intelligence

One row per City × Meeting Store, meeting-raised deals only. Store Visit ≠ Meeting Raised ≠ Store Called — see column definitions below.
Meetings Assigned = deals with Meeting Raised, grouped to this City/Meeting Store. Store Visits = action='Store Visit' AND show_no_show='Show' (genuine confirmed attendance). Visit% = Store Visits / Meetings Assigned. Store Called = meetings_sheet_sync.call_status IS NOT NULL (a distinct signal — a deal can be Store Called without a confirmed Visit, or vice versa). Store Call% = Store Called / Meetings Assigned. Sales = mobile-matched sale on/after the meeting/visit date. Meeting→Sale% = Sales / Meetings Assigned. Rows with no resolved Meeting Store are grouped as "(No Store Resolved)" — never guessed.
